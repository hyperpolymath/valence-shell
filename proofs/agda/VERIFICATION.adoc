== What changed to get there

* *`funext` removed.* Whole-filesystem equalities are now pointwise
  (`_≗_`). Every former `funext helper` proof was already pointwise inside,
  so the theorems keep their content and lose the postulate. Composition uses
  two new congruence lemmas, `fsUpdate-cong` and `applyOp-cong` /
  `applySequence-cong`.
* *K removed.* Clauses that matched `refl` on `p ≡ p` (from `p path-≟ p`)
  now use `fsUpdate-at`, which needs no K.
* *`IsEmptyDir` completed.* It had only an `isDir` field. It now also has
  `noChildren`: no strict descendant (`p ⊑ child`, `child ≢ p`) exists. The
  descendant direction matches Coq and the fixed Lean model.
* *Non-vacuity witnesses.* `PreconditionWitnesses` gives an inhabitant of the
  mkdir, rmdir, createFile, deleteFile and symlink preconditions.

== Warnings

The check prints `UnsupportedIndexedMatch` warnings. They come from matching
`yes` / `no` on the standard library's `Dec`, whose `Reflects` index
needs constructor injectivity that Cubical Agda does not support. The
consequence is that these functions would not compute under cubical
transport. Nothing here is used from Cubical Agda, so they do not affect the
result. Removing them means replacing `Dec` with a non-indexed decision
type throughout; that is recorded as optional clean-up, not done.
