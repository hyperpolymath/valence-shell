| T-1 | Clause form vs the A7 split form | The descriptiles are clauses of `valence-shell_chora.deed`, as the absolute-zero precedent and Hypatia SD004 name them. The *more elegant long-term option*, and the current mapping specs' stated target, is the A7 split: `.machine_readable/descriptiles/{META,ECOSYSTEM,AGENTIC,NEUROSYM}.deed` (`descriptile-deed`, J30, J33, J35), with `(playbook …)` staying in the chora deed. Owner decision: when to move to the split form.
