= Archived proof trees: Isabelle/HOL and Mizar
:toc: preamble

These trees moved here from `proofs/isabelle/` and `proofs/mizar/` on 2026-11-09,
by owner ruling (see `docs/audits/2026-11-09-proof-ground-truth.adoc`). They are
kept, never deleted.

*Why.* Under the estate proof policy (`hyperpolymath/standards`
`3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc`) Agda is the theory source, rechecked in Rocq and
Lean. Isabelle is supplementary evidence only (§3) and Mizar is a §4 exception.
Neither could be run in the 2026-11-09 audit (Isabelle's distribution server was
unavailable; Mizar's installer was not permitted in the session), and neither had
a CI lane. They are *unchecked*: nothing below says these files were ever accepted
by their checker.

*What still uses them.* Nothing. The Justfiles, `scripts/verify-proofs.sh` and
`scripts/validate-with-echidna.sh` were updated in the same change.

today. Matching is *by name* (case, `_` and `-` ignored; Mizar's `Th_` prefix
dropped), so a match says where to look, not that the two statements were
compared. Statement comparison is the §2.2 reconciliation work and is open. The
Agda column is the source of truth; `lean:` and `rocq:` are recheck legs.
